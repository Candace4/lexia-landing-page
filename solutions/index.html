Finance solutions

Home Loans

Whether you are buying your first home, moving into your next property or reviewing your borrowing options, Lexia can help you understand the lending process and explore a solution suited to your circumstances.

Investment Loans

Property investment often requires a different lending strategy. Lexia can help you review finance options with your wider investment goals, borrowing position and future plans in mind.

Refinancing

Your current loan may no longer suit your needs. Lexia can help you review your existing finance, understand the available alternatives and determine whether refinancing may be worthwhile.

Construction Finance

Construction lending involves staged payments and additional requirements. Lexia can guide you through the process and help you understand the finance structure for a new build or substantial renovation.

Asset Finance

Lexia can assist with finance options for vehicles, equipment and other eligible assets, helping individuals and businesses consider a structure that supports their needs and cash flow.

Business Lending

From working capital to business growth and equipment purchases, Lexia can help business owners explore suitable finance options and understand the application process.
